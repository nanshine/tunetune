# Image generation and editing

Clarify identity, style, composition, fidelity and editing constraints before choosing LoRA, DreamBooth, full adaptation, distillation or reward-based improvement. Compare the unchanged pretrained system under the same prompts and sampling conditions. Separate aesthetic preference, identity preservation, prompt compliance and diversity; a single attractive sample is not quality evidence. Record training-image rights and required storage, and inspect overfitting or copying.

Official starting points: [Diffusers training](https://huggingface.co/docs/diffusers/main/en/training/overview), [LoRA](https://huggingface.co/docs/diffusers/main/training/lora).

Map reviewed: 2026-11-03. Before execution verify current task support, model license, revision, platform compatibility, data requirements and resource estimates from the selected official sources. These examples are not a compatibility guarantee.
