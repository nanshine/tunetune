# Graphs and spatial models

Separate graph prediction, point-cloud understanding, scene reconstruction and 3D asset generation: they do not share one fine-tuning recipe. Investigate graph networks for relational structure, NeRF or Gaussian splatting for reconstruction and appropriate pretrained generative models for assets. Pin coordinate conventions, scale, cameras and topology. Split by scene/object where required and measure geometry, appearance and downstream usability separately; a good render does not establish valid geometry.

Official starting points: [PyTorch Geometric](https://pytorch-geometric.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html), [Nerfstudio Splatfacto](https://docs.nerf.studio/nerfology/methods/splat.html), [TRELLIS.2](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2).

Map reviewed: 2026-11-03. Before execution verify current task support, model license, revision, platform compatibility, data requirements and resource estimates from the selected official sources. These examples are not a compatibility guarantee.
