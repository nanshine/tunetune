# Speech and audio

Separate recognition, speech synthesis, speaker tasks, audio classification and speech-language behavior. Compare an unchanged pretrained system before domain adaptation. Inspect language, accent, noise, sample rate and latency; split by speaker and recording origin when independence requires it. Recognition error, synthesis intelligibility, speaker similarity and listener preference are distinct measurements. Protect identifying voice data and clarify rights for voice adaptation.

Official starting points: [NeMo Speech](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/speech/latest/), [ASR fine-tuning](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo/speech/nightly/asr/fine_tuning.html).

Map reviewed: 2026-11-03. Before execution verify current task support, model license, revision, platform compatibility, data requirements and resource estimates from the selected official sources. These examples are not a compatibility guarantee.
